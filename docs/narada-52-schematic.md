# Narada 52-cell exploratory module

The historical scenario is `examples/narada-52-schematic.json`.
Regenerate it with `python3 scripts/generate_narada_52.py`.
Run the historical lumped Python reference or the current CUDA viewer with:

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m battery_sim run examples/narada-52-schematic.json --output results/narada-52
./run_viewer.sh examples/narada-52-schematic.json
```

These are different spatial models. In this historical scene CUDA uses 8 x 4 x 8 thermal
volumes per battery (13,312 total), with an explicitly provisional isotropic
bulk conductivity of 1 W/(m K). Internal gradients and center-to-contact
resistances change propagation relative to the old lumped model. This historical
spatial variant differs from the regression-fitted multiscale default below.
Use `--mesh-scale 2` after the scenario path for 106,496 volumes; see
[CUDA model](cuda-spatial-model.md) and [performance notes](performance.md).

For a controlled reproduction of the old single-temperature experiment, run:

```bash
./run_viewer.sh examples/narada-52-schematic.json --mesh-scale 2 --isothermal
```

The CUDA volume grid is retained, but internal redistribution is instantaneous
and the internal center-to-face resistance vanishes. Contact resistance and its
l/l0 law remain. This is a distinct model limit, not a fitted conductivity.
On both 13,312- and 106,496-volume grids, the isothermal regression reproduces
all 52 historical ignition times to within 1e-7 s and reaches complete
destruction at 1190.2 s. These are old-model regression values, not measured
experimental timings.

The scenario has a hard horizon of 2400 s. The viewer's `All cells destroyed`
auto-pause does not override it. A diagnostic spatial run at `--mesh-scale 2`
had 32 batteries reached / 13 fully spent at the horizon, with 19 still active;
extending only the horizon gave all 52 spent at about 5288.4 s. Thus reaching
the configured time limit does not establish that propagation has stopped.

Spatial results are not grid-converged: at 2400 s, scales 1 / 2 / 4 gave
17 / 32 / 36 batteries reached and 5 / 13 / 17 fully spent. Halving the time
step from 0.2 to 0.1 s at scale 2 left both counts unchanged and shifted onset
times by at most 1.3 s. Spatial resolution and the provisional material/kinetic
model must be addressed before interpreting propagation times quantitatively.

## Multiscale variant and numerical-reference calibration

The default viewer uses `examples/narada-52-multiscale.json`, generated by
`scripts/generate_narada_multiscale.py`. The same 52-battery layout and primary
failure are retained. Each of the 87 direct paths is replaced by a heat-storing
pad and two surface contacts: 416 battery zones + 34,416 pad volumes,
174 contacts. No direct battery-to-battery bypass or heat path through empty
space is added. Battery zones retain finite k=1000 W/m/K, not the isothermal
constraint. Pad lateral faces are adiabatic; volumetric C remains 1.2 MJ/m3/K.

At the user's request, this profile is now fitted to the FIRST LUMPED MODEL,
not to experimental temperature measurements. The reference and parameters
are in `data/calibration/narada-lumped-reference.json` and
`data/calibration/narada-reference-fit.json`. Measured `observations` remain empty.

The original multiscale priors are retained in
`examples/narada-52-multiscale-prior.json` (regenerate with `--prior --output ...`).
That variant added large pad resistances and instantaneous damage coupling from
failed batteries into contacts; it reached only the initiating battery by 6000 s.
It is not the currently fitted default.

### What was fitted

Pad normal k is 5 W/m/K along a broad-face path and 50 W/m/K across a row
bridge; tangential k remains 0.2 W/m/K. These are effective numerical-surrogate
parameters, NOT measured properties of actual insulating pads. Cold and active
surface-contact conductances are fitted separately, accounting for the extra
series pad and battery center-to-face resistances. A single multiplier cannot
match both regimes. Local contact damage onset is 850 K / 950 K, respectively.

Six shared parameters (cold/active G and phase-transition temperature for the
two contact families) were optimized against all 51 secondary ignition times.
Screening used smaller tangential pad meshes, with unchanged normal resolution.
Acceptance was then checked on the FULL 34,832-volume mesh. No per-battery
timing parameters, secondary ignition schedules, artificial source changes or
changes to battery heat capacity/degradation kinetics were introduced.

The contact l/l0 law, first-order transition, hysteresis and thermal damage
remain active on each local contact patch. Extra pad bulk phase variation and
direct endpoint-damage coupling are disabled **in this reference-fit profile**:
the old lumped model cannot independently identify those new degrees of freedom.
Passive damage still accumulates. The engine and the saved prior scenario retain
all those mechanisms for later material-data calibration.

### Measured numerical agreement

Default full grid, 1 s outer intervals, adaptive CUDA substeps:

| Synthetic-reference event | Old lumped model, s | Fitted multiscale CUDA, s |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| First secondary ignition | 213.4 | 204.73 |
| Last ignition in initiating row | 690.6 | 692.10 |
| All 52 batteries fully spent | 1190.2 | 1194.60 |

All 52 batteries run away. Every battery in the initiating row ignites before
any battery in another row; mirror symmetry is retained. RMS onset error over
51 secondaries is 3.69 s, maximum 9.47 s. The fine within-row event ordering is
not exactly identical (e.g. the central vs neighboring cells in the next row).
Full report: `data/calibration/2026-10-12-reference-fit-report.json`.
The original 208 MJ source budget is conserved; relative energy residual is
1.03e-14. This is regression against another model, **not experimental validation**,
and does not establish that the fitted material parameters are unique.

Reducing max_dt to 0.0075 s doubles the actual substep count (93,882 to 187,680).
All 52 still fail, event times change by at most 0.514 s, and completion shifts
from 1194.60 to 1194.95 s. This is a temporal sensitivity check, not proof of
spatial or experimental convergence. The small-dt report is saved alongside
the default report.

An early-window spatial sensitivity run (400 s, pads refined 2x in every axis,
275,744 total volumes) reaches the same primary and four secondary batteries.
Relative to the default mesh, first-neighbor onset shifts by 0.004 s and
second-neighbor onset by 2.14 s. This checks the early front only, not convergence
of the full 52-battery cascade. Its saved report explicitly has partial-window
scope and no full-cascade acceptance verdict.

The default horizon is 2400 s; regression comparison stops at 1400 s, after
complete destruction. Fitting event times alone does not validate temperature
histories, failure physics, other SOCs or other module geometries.

```bash
# Full-grid verification; Python scores events, the native CUDA executable simulates.
python3 scripts/calibrate_narada_reference.py --probe ./build/battery_sim_reference_probe --check

# Optional refitting (SciPy required only here); full-grid verification follows the fit.
python3 scripts/calibrate_narada_reference.py --probe ./build/battery_sim_reference_probe \
  --fit --output results/narada-refit.json

# Time sensitivity; the normal CFL cap is about 0.015 s, so 0.1 s is not a refinement.
python3 scripts/calibrate_narada_reference.py --probe ./build/battery_sim_reference_probe \
  --max-dt 0.0075 --check --output results/narada-small-dt.json
```

Refitting writes a report, not an automatic overwrite of the committed profile.
Review new parameters before editing the profile and regenerating the scene.
`native_reference_fit` is also a CTest. Its tolerances are RMS <=10 s,
maximum <=25 s, completion error <=20 s, all 52 reached, correct row-first
sequence, one primary at 5 s, and relative energy residual <1e-10.

## Public evidence

[Intertek 230400637SHA-002, 22 July 2023](https://www.province-sud.nc/logicweb/logic/document/Ressource/8a8186bb8edd2156018ede8632000057)
is the partner's unit-test reference. Its public two-page summary reports one
externally heated initiating cell and 51 additional runaway cells, with no
propagation to other modules or units. The original report declares 67 pages;
the internal arrangement, initiation location, and temperature histories are
absent from this attachment.

The [manufacturer's public technical proposal, V4](https://www.province-sud.nc/logicweb/logic/document/Ressource/8a8186bb8edd2156018ede8709ac0181)
gives family specifications: 52S1P, LFP, 280 Ah, 5.5 kg per cell,
174 x 72 x 207 mm per cell, and a 760 x 1150 x 237 mm module.
Its model is 166.4NESP280; the test summary names 166.4NESP280L.
Dimensions are therefore a provisional family proxy, not verified sample identity.

## Generated geometry and thermal priors

The assumed 4 x 13 arrangement fits the published envelope. Row gaps are 10 mm;
within-row gaps are 2 mm. It has 48 broad-face contacts and 39 transverse
connections. Those connections represent assumed material bridges; their presence
and composition are not established by the public report. Empty space has no
thermal nodes. The 52 cooling ports are effective ambient boundaries.

The generator starts r2-c07 at 5 s; this location/time is an assumption.
It uses 5500 J/K per cell (published family mass times assumed specific heat),
4 MJ per runaway over 60 s, and provisional contact and damage laws.
The 4 MJ is a separate thermal-source prior; nominal electrical energy is not
converted to runaway heat. Initial aging is zero because the experimental SOH
is unknown. All values and omissions are retained in scenario metadata.

This remains experimentally unvalidated; the multiscale variant's numerical
reference fit does not change that. Published outcomes are stored separately and are not used to prescribe
secondary failures or fabricate event times. Matching the total failure count
would not establish accuracy.

Obtain the complete report/temperature histories and confirm the module variant,
layout, trigger and cooling conditions. Fit single-cell thermal capacity/source
first, then contact conductance and the damage law against neighboring
temperature histories and failure delays. A two-page aggregate outcome alone
cannot identify all these parameters. Use a different experiment for validation.

Source URLs, missing values, extraction method, and access terms are retained in
`data/manifests/narada-52-public-case.json`. Direct PDF downloads returned HTTP
403; the web reader supplied the text, so no raw PDF or invented file hash is
stored. The original documents are not redistributed.

For 52 cells the viewer defaults to a higher camera angle and 16 steps/frame.
Click a cell to inspect it; graphs and the interface list initially show only
its neighborhood. The top-of-scene Temperature/Degradation switch stays visible.
